Our story

Built for
the community.

Bowl Arena began when Roger Douglas and several of his Marine Corps friends pooled their resources to create a local recreational facility. The first eight-lane center opened on LeJeune Boulevard in December 1955.

After an electrical fire destroyed the original building in 1960, Roger and Rosemary Douglas rebuilt. The next Bowl Arena opened in 1961 with sixteen lanes. In 1979, the business moved to Western Boulevard and expanded again to thirty-two lanes.

Today, the next generation of the Douglas family continues the family-oriented bowling tradition.
