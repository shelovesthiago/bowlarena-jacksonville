The party itinerary

Eat. Bowl.
Play.

Each party moves through three activities, giving kids time to celebrate, bowl and finish with arcade games and prizes.

01

Party area. Approximately 45 minutes to eat, open presents, have cake and clean up.

02

Bowling. Get shoes, have the lanes set up and bowl for one hour.

03

Game room. Turn in shoes, play arcade games and redeem tickets for prizes.

04

Arrive early. Guests are encouraged to arrive approximately 30 minutes before the scheduled party time.

Party policies

Keep it simple.
Keep it fun.

These policies keep each party on schedule and give staff enough time to prepare for the next group.

01

Outside food

Booked parties may bring cake and ice cream only. No other outside food or drinks are permitted.

02

Decorations

Keep decorations minimal enough to set up and remove within 15 minutes. Balloons must be weighted or tied down.

03

No messy items

Do not bring confetti, silly string, bubbles or anything else that creates a difficult cleanup.

04

Extra guests

Extra guests and additional food items are paid for on the day of the party.
