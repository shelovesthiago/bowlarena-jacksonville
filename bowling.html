Group reservations

Save space
for the crew.

Reservations are intended for groups needing at least two lanes. Individual lanes remain first come, first served.

Call to request a reservation
01

Two-lane minimum. Reservations are made by the hour with a deposit equal to one hour for each lane.

02

Plan ahead. Same-day reservations are not accepted.

03

Weekend limits. Reservations are not taken Friday or Saturday after 5 PM.

04

Holiday limits. Reservations are not taken for holidays or holiday weekends.

05

Cancellations. Cancel at least 24 hours before the reservation for a full refund.

06

Pay the balance later. Additional hours and shoe rentals are paid on the day of the reservation.

Open bowling policy

Arrive together.
Bowl sooner.

Lanes are assigned once an entire party is present and ready to bowl. If part of your group is missing, fully assembled groups may be assigned ahead of you and your estimated wait can change.

Cash and credit cards are accepted. Tap-to-pay is not currently available.
